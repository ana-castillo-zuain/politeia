• Other new or modified information
– Changes to the Measurement Model
∗ We have changed the prior distribution for universal thresholds γkµ in the V-Dem
measurement model from a uniform distribution ( U(-6,6)) to a normal distribution
(N(0,6)). We found that the uniform priors were too restrictive and often too uninfor-
mative within their bounds to identify unique stationary distributions. After extensive
analyses, we found that relatively wide normal distibutions provide roughly identical
point and uncertainty estimates for latent country-year values as models that use uni-
form distributions, and tend to converge at a higher rate. Nevertheless, data sparsity
still creates diffculties for the identification of a unique stationary distribution for
some variables. See Pemstein et al. (2026) for further information about the V-Dem
Measurement Model.
– Regimes Survey
∗ The Regimes survey was not updated in v15, but has been updated in v16. Regime
variables now have data up to 2025.
– Coder-level Datasets
∗ The coder-level datasets have been re-uploaded, and coder ids have been changed.
Coder ids remain comparable across coder-level dataset versions.
